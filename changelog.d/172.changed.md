**Proxy hot path: one fetch per failed flight, index rebuilds held back, leak scan without parsing (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#172)** — a single-flight leader whose upstream fetch fails now shares that failure (the same status, a 404 stays a 404) with the requests already waiting on it, instead of each of them fetching at once into the failing upstream; nothing is remembered past the flight, and a cancelled leader hands over to one new leader. The UI repository index of a registry whose listing took over 2 s is rebuilt on demand at most once per 20 such durations (capped at the 10 min refresh), not on every 60 s gauge tick while artifacts are being cached. The upstream URL leak detector scans the raw bytes of every JSON response and parses the document only when they name an upstream or carry a `\u00xx` escape, and reads a gzip NuGet registration from the bytes its handler compressed: it catches what it caught, at 71 µs instead of 2.8 ms for a clean 1.4 MB packument.
