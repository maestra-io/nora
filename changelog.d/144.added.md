**Docker quarantine dates cgr.dev digests by the Chainguard tag history (issues-maestra#1649)** — cgr.dev had no date source, so every new digest of a Chainguard image, rebuilt about daily and pinned by Renovate as `:latest@sha256:…`, was held for the TTL from NORA's first fetch (chainguard/nginx `0352063c`, published 07.11 19:55Z, held from 08.11 12:49Z). `trust_upstream_dates` on cgr.dev now dates a digest by `/v2/<repo>/_chainguard/history/<tag>` `updateTimestamp` with an anonymous token: a tag request by that tag's history, a digest request by the history of the repository's first 10 tags (cosign `sha256-*` tags skipped), 1000-record pages followed up to 5 per tag. Platform manifests take their index's date, a walk that outlasts the request backdates when it lands, a pull by digest of a cached held manifest asks again; a failed lookup leaves first-seen.
