**Docker: a tag delete keeps a digest other tags share; tags/list pages; only manifests are stored and cached (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#156)** — deleting one tag removed the digest-keyed manifest that every other tag of the same digest is pulled by; it now stays until its last tag goes. `tags/list` is sorted, honours `n` and `last` with a `Link` header and answers 404 `NAME_UNKNOWN` for a repository NORA holds nothing of (before: the whole list, and 200 with no tags). A pushed body that is not a `schemaVersion` 2 JSON object, or whose `mediaType` differs from the `Content-Type`, is 400 `MANIFEST_INVALID`, and the tag is written after the digest copy. An upstream manifest is read under the 4 MiB push cap and a 200 that is not a manifest is a failed upstream, not a cached tag. On the fallback chain every copy of one fetch is cached under one namespace.
