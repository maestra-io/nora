**Tests: the npm published-name tests no longer hang CI now and then.** wiremock's `MockServer` verifies its mocks on drop with `futures::executor::block_on`, which polls tokio locks on the test's runtime thread; once a test had spent its tokio coop budget, those locks deferred their wake-up to the scheduler parked inside that same `block_on`, and the test waited forever (16 of 2400 contended runs of the module; in CI it held a runner for 84 minutes). The tests' mock upstreams now shut down on their own thread (`test_helpers::MockUpstream`), and the `cargo test`, `helm chart`, `cargo deny`, `coherence` and `changes` CI jobs got a `timeout-minutes`. No production code path is involved.
