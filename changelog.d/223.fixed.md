**An enforcing quarantine no longer refuses proxy fetches and Docker pulls while the ledger loads after a restart** — #212 fixed this for cache hits only. A file fetched from the upstream (every registry), and a Docker blob or manifest fetched from its upstream, whose digest had no record in memory yet was still answered 403 for up to 10 minutes after a restart, though the ledger may hold it as mature; Docker treats that 403 as final. These now wait for the load, at most 30 s, and past that answer 503 with `Retry-After: 30` (Docker: an OCI `UNAVAILABLE` error), recording nothing. Observe mode is unchanged.
