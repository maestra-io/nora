**`nora_storage_operation_duration_seconds` histogram** — every storage backend call (`get`, `put`, `get_reader`, `get_range`, `list`, `list_with_meta`, `stat`, `pin`, `delete`, `copy`) now records its wall-clock under `operation`. `nora_storage_operations_total` only counted the calls, so an object store that answered slowly but without errors was invisible: two `NoraHighLatency` firings (23.10 and 02.11.2026) ended with the stall cause not established. `get`/`put` cover the whole body, `get_reader`/`get_range` only the open (the stream is paced by the download client), `list*` the whole walk.
