**Buffered publish bodies share one memory budget, and are read only after the scope check (audit A072, A073)** — the npm, Maven, Cargo, deb and rpm publish bodies, read whole into memory, were each capped at `publish_body_limit_mb` but nothing bounded them together, and a stalled body held its buffer for as long as the connection lived. `[server] publish_inflight_mb` (`NORA_PUBLISH_INFLIGHT_MB`, default 2048, 0 = no budget) now bounds them together: a publish that would pass it is answered 503 with `Retry-After: 10` as its body arrives (`nora_spool_failures_total{reason="publish_busy"}`), a body bigger than the whole budget is a 413, and a body that sends nothing for 60 s is a 408. Maven, npm and deb/rpm decide the namespace scope of the URL before they read the body (an out-of-scope oversized publish is now 403, not 413), and the anonymous `npm login` PUT is answered 405 before any of its body is read.
