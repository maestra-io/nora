**Docker quarantine dates a tag's digest when the registry walk outlasts the request (issues-maestra#1649)** — ghcr.io, mcr.microsoft.com and public.ecr.aws date a digest from a walk of the whole repository, and a request waited 5 s for it: the first request of a repository, usually a tag, recorded first-seen while the digest request a second later had the registry's date (gha-runner-scale-set-controller 0.15.1 on 05.11; 23 ghcr and 8 public.ecr.aws first sights in a day). A walk that lands after the request stopped waiting now moves the digest, and what it names (public.ecr.aws's untagged platform manifests take their index's date), back to the registry's date; ghcr pages after the first are fetched at once; a digest pull of a cached held manifest asks the walked registries again. A date moved back is written as `<entry>.dated` beside the ledger entry and every start takes the earliest, so it outlives a restart (it lived in memory, and the boot snapshot brought NORA's date back).
