**Docker: a blob `DELETE` and a GC sweep can no longer leave a pushed manifest over a missing layer (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#217)** — a manifest push checked its layers with a bare `stat`, and a sweep or a blob `DELETE` between that check and the manifest write deleted a layer of a manifest that was then stored. The push now checks each referenced blob and child manifest the way blob `HEAD` does (`gc.confirm_blob`), so the sweep keeps it for `gc.grace_secs`. A blob `DELETE` takes the publish locks of both copies and answers 409 `DENIED` for a blob confirmed to a client within `gc.grace_secs` (a `HEAD` 200, a mount, a manifest push that referenced it); with `gc.enabled = false` it behaves as before.
