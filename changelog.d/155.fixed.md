**Auth hardening from review round 4 (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#155)** — with `public_metrics = false`, `/metrics` now needs real credentials even under `anonymous_read` (any `Authorization` header used to open it); OIDC validation enforces `nbf` and holds a token without `iat` to `max_token_lifetime_secs` by the lifetime it has left (it escaped the ceiling); a token revoke that cannot reach the store answers 503 instead of "Token not found" while the token kept working; an `nra_` token sent to `/` or a static asset is no longer looked up in storage; Argon2 verify and hash run on the blocking pool and concurrent cache misses of one token do one storage read and one hash (they ran on the async workers, once per request); cached token verdicts past the TTL are dropped on the maintenance tick.
