**A body that outlives the proxy timeout no longer trips the shared upstream breaker (Maestra fork, audit A026)** — the buffered fetchers (`proxy_fetch` and its text/bytes forms, the conditional NuGet/npm revalidation, the npm audit POST forward) counted a 2xx whose body did not finish within `proxy_timeout` as an upstream failure, and the breaker is one counter per format or per configured upstream: a few large or slow objects answered 503 to every other reader of that format until `reset_timeout`. Now an upstream that sent its 2xx headers in time is alive to the breaker (no change in Closed, a half-open probe closes it), and a debug line marks the case. A connect or headers timeout, a mid-body drop, a 5xx after the retry, a 429, transport errors and the idle stall of the streaming path still count as failures. The reader whose body timed out gets the same error as before. Trade-off: an upstream that sends headers fast but trickles every body no longer fails fast; each cold miss waits one `proxy_timeout`. That format's `proxy_timeout` bounds the wait.
