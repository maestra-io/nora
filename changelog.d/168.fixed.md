**NuGet metadata: a stale copy has an age bound, a 304 without a cached body recovers (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#168)** — with `serve_stale`, a version list or registration document past `metadata_ttl` was answered as it was and refreshed behind the response whatever its age, so the first restore of a version published during an idle period failed with NU1102; `[nuget] max_stale` (`NORA_NUGET_MAX_STALE`, default 3600 s, `0` = always revalidate first, `-1` = no bound) now limits that, and an older copy is revalidated before the answer. A 304 for a document whose cached body is gone drops the validators and fetches it whole instead of answering 502 for as long as the upstream says 304. Registration documents above 64 KiB are rewritten and gzipped on the blocking pool.
