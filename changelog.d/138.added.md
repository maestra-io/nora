**Break-glass exceptions to the docker digest quarantine (Maestra fork, issues-maestra#1649)** — `[docker] quarantine_exceptions` names a TOML file of `[[exception]]` entries (`digest`, `reason`, `expires`). nora re-reads it every 30 s, with no restart. An unexpired entry admits that manifest digest, and every digest an admitted manifest names, under `observe` and `enforce`. The manifest answer then says `x-nora-quarantine: exception`. Each use is logged and counted in `nora_docker_quarantine_exception_used_total{digest}`, and alert `NoraQuarantineExceptionUsed` fires on it. A file with an entry that has no reason, a malformed or repeated digest, or an expiry more than 7 days after the read is refused whole. nora keeps the last good list, logs the error and sets `nora_docker_quarantine_exceptions_invalid` (alert `NoraQuarantineExceptionsInvalid`). Before, the only way past a hold was to wait out `quarantine_ttl`. See docs/docker-proxy.md.
