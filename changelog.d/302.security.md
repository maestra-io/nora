**Docker blob proxy fills draw on a spool budget; a range on a cache miss gets only verified bytes (Maestra fork, audits A060, A061)** — a proxied blob fill spooled into `{storage.path}/tmp/docker-proxy` outside every bound: no byte budget, no free-space floor, no limit on concurrent fills, and it kept downloading after its client left. A new `docker.proxy_spool_max_mb` (env `NORA_DOCKER_PROXY_SPOOL_MAX_MB`) is that budget: each fill reserves room before upstream is asked, grows within the budget by the declared length and before every write, and holds it until the blob is stored and every response reading the spool is done (an open read handle keeps an unlinked spool on disk); the spool's 64 MiB free-space floor now applies to Docker fills too, budget or not. Separately, a `Range` request answered from a fill in flight released bytes before the fill's digest was verified, so a range ending before the last byte could complete on unverified bytes; it now gets its 206 headers at once and its first body byte only after the whole blob verified, and a failed fill fails it before any byte. Operators: the default 0 keeps fills unbudgeted (a warning at start says so); once set, a cold layer larger than the budget is a 507 and a fill while the budget is held by others is a 503 with `Retry-After` (`nora_spool_failures_total{registry="docker"}`), followers of a running fill take no budget. The chart refuses a value at or above the ephemeral-storage limit when `/data` is an emptyDir. A resumed pull (`Range: bytes=N-`) of a blob not yet cached waits for the whole fill before its body flows; cache hits and plain pulls are unchanged.
