**Cold Helm charts, apk packages and GitHub release assets stream as they download (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#192)** — since #176 a cold miss survived a client that gave up, but the client still got no byte until the whole download ended. A GET is now answered once the first upstream chunk is spooled and follows the spool as it grows; the last byte is held until the whole body passed its check (helm and GitHub: the published sha256; apk: the control segment, checked before the first byte), and a body that fails it, or a fill that breaks off, aborts the transfer instead of ending it cleanly, and is not cached. For helm and GitHub, curation and the quarantine decide on the published digest before the fetch; apk has no published whole-package digest, so they decide on the sha256 of the whole body once read. A request joining a running fill, a HEAD, a GitHub asset without a digest and an apk package a whole-file gate could refuse (curation integrity pin, quarantine `enforce`) are fetched whole first, as before.
