**Cold Helm charts, apk packages and GitHub release assets stream as they download (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#192)** — since #176 a cold miss survived a client that gave up, but the client still got no byte until the whole download ended. A GET is now answered once the first upstream chunk is spooled and follows the spool as it grows; the last byte is held until the whole body passed its check (helm and GitHub: the published sha256; apk: the control segment, checked before the first byte), and a body that fails it, or a fill that breaks off, aborts the transfer instead of ending it cleanly, and is not cached. Curation and the quarantine decide on the published digest before the fetch. A request joining a running fill, a HEAD, a GitHub asset without a digest and an apk package a whole-file gate could refuse (curation integrity pin, quarantine `enforce`) are fetched whole first, as before.
