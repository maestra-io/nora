**An enforcing quarantine no longer refuses cached packages while the ledger loads after a restart** — the ledger loads in the background for up to 10 minutes, and until then a cache hit on a copy younger than 90 days had no record and was answered 403 as first seen now, though the ledger may hold it as mature (a `dotnet restore` failed on packages served for months). Such a hit now waits for the load, at most 30 s, and past that answers 503 with `Retry-After: 30`; it is never served unchecked. Observe mode is unchanged.
