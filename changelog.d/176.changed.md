**Proxy hot path: one SHA-256 per cache hit, a cap on buffered upstream bodies, artifact downloads that survive a client disconnect (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#176)** — a cache hit hashed the same body three to five times (the storage gate, its witness, curation, quarantine, the npm sidecar check), all but one on the async workers; the storage gate now verifies once on the blocking pool and every later check uses the digest it matched (hashing a 1 MiB body four times against once in a synthetic bench: 1.33 ms against 0.33 ms; not a measured request latency). `[server] proxy_max_buffered_mb` (`NORA_PROXY_MAX_BUFFERED_MB`, default 512, 0 = none) refuses with 507 an upstream body read whole into memory (the metadata of every format, and the artifacts of the formats that do not spool) above the cap, instead of buffering a multi-GB document until the process is OOM-killed. A cold miss of a NuGet, npm, PyPI, helm, apk or GitHub artifact is now downloaded in a task of its own (with `server.proxy_coalesce`, the default; without it the download still belongs to its request): a client that times out before the download ends (pip after 15 s) no longer cancels it, its retry joins the same download, and a download that ended with nobody waiting is kept for 120 s for the request that comes back for it (in-memory bodies kept this way share a 256 MiB budget, a download nobody has waited on for 30 minutes is given up, and at most 16 run unattended at a time). Criterion benches for the packument, registration and cached artifact paths (`cargo bench -p nora-registry --bench hotpath`).
