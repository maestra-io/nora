**Maven cache misses no longer wait 30 s for a date, and old Central artifacts are no longer held as new (#160, issues-maestra#1649)** — the Maven Central publish date came only from the search API, on `proxy_timeout` (30 s), and every failure was silent: a throttled or slow search added 30 s to a cold miss and left a years-old artifact on the quarantine's first-seen clock. The date now comes from the file's `Last-Modified` on Central (a HEAD), with the search API as the fallback, both within `maven.date_lookup_timeout` (default 5 s, `NORA_MAVEN_DATE_LOOKUP_TIMEOUT`). Each failure is logged at WARN with its source and reason and counted in `nora_maven_date_lookup_total{source,outcome}`. A cached artifact the quarantine holds is dated again before it is served, once an hour per digest, so an artifact held after a failed lookup matures as soon as Central gives its date. An artifact with no date still goes by first-seen.
