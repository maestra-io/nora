**Release tooling verifies what it downloads and relands only the approved commit (audit A001, A003, A004, A008, A009, A010)** — `dist/install.sh` used to stage under fixed `/tmp` names, move them into place with `sudo mv`, and install the binary even when the signature check failed, was skipped or could not run; it now stages in a private `mktemp -d`, installs with `sudo install` and explicit owner and mode, asks for the published `nora-linux-amd64`/`arm64` assets, and installs only after `cosign verify-blob --bundle` passes (`NORA_SKIP_VERIFY=1` opts out with a warning). Every cosign check of release artifacts (`install.sh`, `post-release-gate.sh`) pins one anchored signer, release.yml on a `v*` tag; the release and packaging workflows check the nfpm tarball by sha256 and no longer reuse an nfpm from the runner's PATH; auto-reland relands the commit of the last approval only while it is the head, fetches that commit instead of the PR ref, runs the FileChanges builder saved from the base, and keeps `RELAND_TOKEN` out of the step that handles PR content. `scripts/coherence-check.sh` now fails on a Dockerfile base without an `@sha256:` digest (the RU-vendor `redos` and `astra` stages are exempt by name) and on any signer identity other than the anchored one. These workflows stay disabled in this fork.
