**`nora_quarantine_holds_total{client}` says who a hold met (Maestra fork, issues-maestra#1649, #313)** — the counter could not tell a Renovate lookup of a fresh tag or the CI of a pull request bumping to it from a node or default-branch pull that is really refused, so an alert on `outcome="blocked"` fired on every Renovate run once the docker quarantine enforced (102 holds in 20 minutes on 23.11.2026, all Mend Renovate). A layer inside auth now classes each request once: `renovate` for a principal listed in the new `[curation] renovate_principals` (`NORA_CURATION_RENOVATE_PRINCIPALS`, chart `config.curation.renovate_principals`), `pull_request` for an OIDC subject ending in `:pull_request`, `consumer` for everyone else, unauthenticated included; every hold site of every registry counts under it, and every hold log line carries `client` and `principal`. Docker's enforce answer of a moved tag with its admitted copy counts `outcome="fallback"` instead of `blocked`: nothing was refused. Metric and log only; the quarantine judges every client alike.
