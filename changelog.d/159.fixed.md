**Docker: chunked uploads honour `Content-Range` and report their status; blob HEAD, DELETE and Range answers (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#159)** — a chunk whose `Content-Range` does not start where the upload stands is 416 with the `Range` held (a retried chunk was appended twice), and `GET` on the upload URL answers 204 with that `Range`. A `PUT` to an upload NORA does not hold is 404 `BLOB_UPLOAD_UNKNOWN` instead of `DIGEST_INVALID`, and a finalize refused for its digest algorithm keeps the upload. `DELETE` of a pushed blob answers 202 with an upstream configured (before: deleted, then 404). A manifest over blobs that `HEAD` reports from the proxy cache can be pushed; they are copied into the pushed layout. `HEAD` of a blob carries `Docker-Content-Digest` and the curation gate of its `GET`. A `Range` request no longer opens a read of the whole blob first.
