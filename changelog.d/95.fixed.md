npm: a proxied packument that has to be parsed (the `min_release_age` filter, the `install-v1` form) is rendered once per document and variant — concurrent requests share the render and it is reused for 60 s within 128 MiB. Before, every request parsed its own copy, and a CI fleet asking for a large packument (`vite`, `@types/node`) at once OOM-killed a 4 GiB instance.
