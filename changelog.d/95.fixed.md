**Proxied npm packuments are rendered once per document (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#95)** — a packument that has to be parsed (the `min_release_age` filter, the `install-v1` form) is keyed by package, variant and the sha256 of the source document; concurrent requests share one render and it is reused for 60 s (the response `max-age`) within 128 MiB. Before, every request parsed its own copy, and a CI fleet asking for `vite` and `@types/node` at once OOM-killed the 4 GiB pod three times in two minutes (packages.maestra.io 502/503, 22.10.2026).
