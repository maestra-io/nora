**Alpine (apk) repository proxy with a release-age hold (Maestra fork, maestra-io/issues-maestra#1649)** — `[apk]` proxies Alpine mirrors at `/apk/<proxy>/<branch>/<repo>/<arch>/`: the upstream `APKINDEX.tar.gz` is verified with the Alpine keys, versions built (`t:`) less than `curation.apk.min_release_age` ago are left out (apk resolves the newest older version nora has cached, or fails with `no such package`), and the index is re-signed with `signing_key_path` (`.SIGN.RSA256.<key_name>`, public key at `/apk/keys/<key_name>`). `.apk` files are served only for listed versions, refused while young, and checked against the index's `C:` identity. See docs/apk-proxy.md.
