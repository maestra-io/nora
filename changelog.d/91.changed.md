**Quarantine ledger loads off the startup path (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#91)** — `DigestStore::load_in_background` returns an empty store at once and merges the ledger when the bucket walk finishes (10 min deadline, fail-open past it); the listener no longer waits on storage. Before, a slow `.nora-quarantine/` listing on R2 kept the port closed and the startup probe restarted the pod in a loop (packages.maestra.io 503, 20.10.2026).
