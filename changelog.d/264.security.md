**Go versions dated by the proxy's own first fetch (Maestra fork, `[go] attested_dates`)** — since #234 (A066) a Go version NORA had not cached before was refused for `min_release_age` from its first fetch, however old (sigs.k8s.io/e2e-framework v0.7.0, released April 2026, refused as "0h old"). With `[go] attested_dates` / `NORA_GO_ATTESTED_DATES=true` (default off) a version is dated by the `Last-Modified` of its `.zip` on `go.proxy` — on proxy.golang.org the proxy's own first fetch (the index.golang.org `Timestamp`), which the publisher cannot set — never earlier than the `.info` `Time`. One HEAD per version on the `#dates` breaker, kept at `go/<module>/@nora-dates/<version>`; a failed lookup keeps nothing and falls back to NORA's first sight.
