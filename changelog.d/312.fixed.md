**A tags/list redirect off the upstream's origin no longer opens the circuit breaker (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#312)** — registry.k8s.io answers every `tags/list` with a 307 to its Artifact Registry mirror. NORA does not follow that hop (R4-R09), but it counted the handed-back 3xx as an upstream failure, so five listings opened the breaker of the whole upstream and uncached k8s images failed fast (NoraCircuitBreakerOpen, 21.11.2026). The 3xx now counts as the upstream answering; the listing still fails with 502.
