**A tags/list redirect off the upstream's origin is followed without credentials and does not open the circuit breaker (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#312)** — registry.k8s.io answers every `tags/list` with a 307 to its Artifact Registry mirror. The same-origin client handed that hop back and NORA counted it as an upstream failure, so five listings opened the breaker of the whole upstream and uncached k8s images failed (NoraCircuitBreakerOpen, 21.11.2026). NORA now takes the hop once, with no credential, and a redirect it does not take counts as the upstream answering.
