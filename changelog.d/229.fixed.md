**A cold-miss artifact dropped before its cache decision goes to the next request (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#229)** — #176 kept the download of a client that left mid-download for its retry, but once the body reached its request the single-flight ended and caching it was that request's job: a client that left while the request's own checks ran (npm's tarball integrity hash and packument refetch, apk's control segment, the verdict of a streamed helm/apk/GitHub miss) lost the body, and its retry downloaded it again. The body now stays in its flight until the request decides; dropped undecided, the next request for the key takes it, runs its checks and caches it (the same 120 s hold and 256 MiB budget as #176). A body the request refused is never handed on, and a request arriving while the first one runs its checks joins its flight instead of starting a second download.
