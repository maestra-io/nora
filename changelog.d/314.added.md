**Docker: `tags/list` leaves out a tag refused for a held digest under enforce (Maestra fork, issues-maestra#1649, #314)** — Renovate has release dates only for Docker Hub, so for ecr-public, ghcr, quay, mcr and gcr it took the newest tag at once and fetched its manifest on every lookup: while the quarantine held the digest each fetch was a 403 (91 on `tbot-distroless:18.11.13` in 20 minutes on 23.11.2026), and it proposed bumps to images nothing could pull yet. Under `[curation.docker] quarantine = "enforce"` a tag NORA answered 403 (digest held, no admitted copy) is now left out of `tags/list` until its digest matures, as npm and helm indexes leave out held versions. A tag not resolved yet stays listed (its first pull starts the clock), as do a tag answered with its admitted copy, a digest a break-glass exception covers and a `quarantine_exempt` repository; `observe` hides nothing; `n`/`last` page the filtered list. The refused tags are kept in memory (at most 65536), so no upstream request is made per tag and a restart lists a tag until its next refusal.
