**npm: a proxied package is ruled out as published from its provenance record, not the document (maestra-io/nora#109)**. To learn that a package is not published here, `published_state` read `npm/<name>/metadata.json` in full on every proxied tarball, packument and audit name. The same key holds the upstream copy a release before 1.4.0 cached, 15–39 MB for `vite`, `typescript` or `@typescript-eslint/*`. In a CI wave on 2026-10-23 that came to 37.9 GB of R2 reads in six minutes and 5.4 GiB RSS. The small record is now read first, and one that does not name `nora-published` decides without the document. In a replay of that peak, storage reads fell from 5228 to 294 MiB and peak RSS from 1083–1561 to 332 MiB.
