**Go release dates bounded by NORA's first sight; unknown dates judged per registry (Maestra fork, audit A066, A067)** — a Go `.zip` was dated only by its upstream `.info` `Time`, the publisher's commit date, and when NORA held no `.info` for the version (a `.zip` asked for first, or a zip cached without one) it was not dated at all and `min_release_age` let it through. The date is now the later of `Time` and when NORA first stored the `.info` (now when that cannot be read), a missing `.info` is fetched from the Go proxy before the download is judged, and a zip cached without one is dated no earlier than its copy. A date still unknown goes through the new `[curation.<registry>] unknown_date` (`NORA_CURATION_<REGISTRY>_UNKNOWN_DATE`): `hold` judges it as released now (NuGet's default, its behaviour so far), `log` leaves it undated with a `[SECURITY] min-release-age unknown date` line and `nora_curation_unknown_release_date_total{registry}` (every other registry's default). Operators: under enforce with a Go `min_release_age`, a Go version NORA has not cached before is refused for that long from its first fetch, however old its `Time`; versions already cached keep their age. Exemptions: an exact `[curation.go] allow` entry, the bypass token, or `[curation.go] min_release_age = "0d"`.
