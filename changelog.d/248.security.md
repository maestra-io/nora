**Raw uploads and Hugging Face cache fills count against the spool budget (audit A080, A069, maestra-io/nora#248)** — a raw `PUT` used to stream to `{storage.path}/tmp/raw-uploads` and an hf cache fill to `{storage.path}/tmp/hf-proxy`, neither counted in `server.spool_max_mb` nor checked against the spool free-space floor, so concurrent transfers could fill the data volume. Both now live under `server.spool_dir` (`raw-uploads/`, `hf-proxy/`), reserve every byte in the shared budget before it is written and are reaped by the spool sweep. A raw `PUT` can now get 503 + `Retry-After` while the budget is in use and 507 past the budget or the floor; a startup warning flags a `raw.max_file_size` above `spool_max_mb`. An hf fill the budget or `proxy_max_artifact_mb` cannot hold is served whole and checked, just not cached. `spool_max_mb = 0` keeps the old unbounded behaviour; the old raw temp directory is still swept for one release.
