**npm: a proxied tarball is checked against its packument; publish validates the name (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#164)** — a tarball fetched from an upstream is compared with `dist.integrity` (sha512, else `dist.shasum`) of its version in the upstream's packument before it is served or cached: a mismatch is a 502 and nothing is stored, the packument fetched again once when the cached copy disagrees. Before, a truncated or wrong body was cached for good with a self-consistent `.sha256` and every `npm ci` failed with EINTEGRITY until the key was deleted by hand. `npm publish` refuses a URL path that is not `name` or `@scope/name` (`foo/versions` wrote among the keys of `foo`). A cached tarball verified against its storage pin is no longer read against the `.sha256` sidecar again, and the `versions/` stat runs together with the first-seen ledger read: two storage round trips before the tarball instead of four.
