**Docker quarantine dates what a manifest names at the manifest's first-seen (#112)** — under `quarantine = "enforce"` a multi-arch image was held one level at a time (index, then the platform manifest a client asks for only once the index is served, then config and layers): three `quarantine_ttl`s, nine days at `3d`. A proxied manifest now records every digest it names at its own first-seen (NORA's clock, never an upstream date; a digest already recorded keeps its own), so an image waits one TTL and a single manifest request starts the clock for the whole tree.
