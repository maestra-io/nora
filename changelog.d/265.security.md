**A connection whose client stops sending its body or reading the response is closed (audit A087, A071)** — past the request head a connection had no deadline: a client that sent part of a request body and went silent, or never read a large response, kept its `server.max_connections` place, and a spooled response its spool file, for as long as it stayed connected. The new `server.body_idle_timeout` (`NORA_BODY_IDLE_TIMEOUT`, default 120 s, `0` = off) closes a connection once its client has moved nothing for that long, for HTTP/1 and HTTP/2; each close is logged and counted in `nora_connections_stalled_total`. It is an idle window, not a total: slow transfers that move within each window, and time NORA spends itself (handlers, upstream fetches, spool fills), are never cut.
