**Docker quarantine: a held Hub/quay digest is dated by a pull of its tag, and a refused ledger write is retried (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#310)** — a digest first pulled by digest whose tag sat past the 3×100 searched Docker Hub/quay tags stayed held by NORA's first-seen even when a later pull of its tag could date it with one request, because only digest-dating registries were asked again (otel/opentelemetry-collector-contrib:0.116.1, pushed December 2024, was refused for three days on iota). A tag pull of a held digest now asks Hub and quay again. A new ledger entry the store failed to take was never written again, so a pod that started without the ledger (R2 403s on 18.11.2026) held old artifacts by its own clock until restart; the write is now retried (30 s, doubling to 10 min, for up to 6 h) and the stored, earlier entry adopted.
