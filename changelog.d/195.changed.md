**PyPI: an uploaded project owns its name (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#195, R4-R04)**: the first twine upload of a project outside `curation.internal_namespaces` now makes it hosted, as `nora import` does. Before, `/simple/<project>/` merged the uploaded files with whatever a configured upstream listed under the same name, so a higher version registered on pypi.org won `pip install` (dependency confusion). Now its index (HTML and PEP 691) and its files are served from its own files only, and the name is no longer sent upstream. Earlier uploads stay listed. Files cached from an upstream under the name before that upload are no longer listed or served. The `[SECURITY]` WARN and `nora_pypi_mixed_project_uploads_total{upstream_lists}` now fire once, on the upload that claims the name. Internal-namespace projects are unchanged.
