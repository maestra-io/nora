**GC and retention fail closed (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#153)** — a failed `stat`, a failed listing or a refused delete now keeps the object and is counted (`nora_gc_list_failures_total`, `nora_gc_delete_failures_total`, `nora_retention_skipped_unknown_total`, `nora_retention_delete_failures_total`). Before, retention read a failed `stat` as mtime 0 and deleted a tag pushed minutes ago as "older than 30 days"; GC judged a sidecar orphan on a timed-out HEAD of its primary, removed an npm version published during the pass from the packument, counted refused deletes as evicted, deleted proxy-cached Go versions that had no `.zip`, and could sweep a layer a pushing client had just been told exists (a blob `HEAD` or mount now protects it for `gc.grace_secs`, in memory, one replica). Retention takes age and size from one listing per registry instead of a HEAD per key, and rebuilds an npm packument or an rpm/deb index right after its group instead of at the end of the pass. `gc.interval = 0` with GC enabled, or `retention.interval = 0` with retention or NuGet enabled, is now a config error (it was a busy loop).
