**A Maven artifact over the in-memory cap is spooled, and a body NORA refuses is never a 404 (Maestra fork, maestra-io/nora#186)** — Maven proxy artifacts were buffered whole under `server.proxy_max_buffered_mb` (512 MiB): `software/amazon/awssdk/bundle/2.29.52/bundle-2.29.52.jar` (641 MB) was refused on Central, maven.google.com and Cloudsmith answered "not found", and the miss was a silent 404. Maven artifacts above `server.stream_threshold_mb` are now spooled to disk and served and cached from the spool, as NuGet/npm/PyPI files are, with the integrity check and the digest quarantine on the spooled body's digest. A miss where NORA refused an upstream's body (size cap, spool busy, local disk) answers with the spool's status (507, 503 + `Retry-After`, 500), whatever another upstream said, and every proxy miss logs a `proxy miss` WARN with each upstream's answer.
